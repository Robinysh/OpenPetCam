Open Pet Cam: a DIY pet cam you own

Short answer: Open Pet Cam (written OpenPetCam) is a free, open-source pet cam you put together yourself. A cheap pan/tilt Wi-Fi camera runs open Thingino firmware, a small server you own runs the software, and your phone gets live video, a camera that goes looking for your dog, and alerts. There's no subscription and no vendor cloud.

What a pet cam should do

Most pet cams are home security cameras with a pet label: they record a fixed view and send motion alerts. A dog doesn't stay in one view. Open Pet Cam is built around the pet instead:

  • Live video with sound on your phone, plus what she's doing: resting, active or out of view
  • It finds her. When she leaves the picture, the camera pans and tilts to search the room until it sees her again, or you tap Find
  • It knows her from her toys. A small model trained on your own camera's pictures checks every sighting
  • It keeps her day: naps, activity, barks and short clips on a timeline, a nightly highlight reel and a timelapse
  • It tells you when it matters: barking, distress sounds, camera offline, view blocked

How it works

The camera dials out to your server over an encrypted WireGuard tunnel, so nothing is opened on your home network. The server keeps a rolling recording, runs pet detection, steers the camera, sends push notifications and serves the app, which you add to your phone's Home Screen. Video goes from your camera to your server and nowhere else. One tap turns the picture and microphone off.

Setting it up

  1. Flash the camera with Thingino and join it to your Wi-Fi.
  2. On your computer: git clone https://github.com/crimeacs/OpenPetCam.git && cd OpenPetCam && ./setup. It asks for your server's address, installs everything and tells you what to type into the camera's WireGuard page.
  3. Open the printed address on your phone, sign in, and add it to the Home Screen.

The Thingino pet camera guide covers each step in detail.

Trade-offs

You do the setup yourself, and you should be comfortable in a terminal. You flash open firmware onto the camera. Open Pet Cam is built and tested for dogs first. There's no treat tosser. For a side-by-side with commercial pet cams, see the Furbo and Petcube comparison.

Questions

Is Open Pet Cam the same as OpenPetCam?

Yes. It's one project; the name is usually written as one word.

Does it work with cats?

Dogs come first: the sound model and the default verifier are tuned for dogs. Other pets haven't been tested yet.

Can I use a camera I already have?

If it's a pan/tilt model that Thingino supports, yes. Check the camera list on the Thingino site before you buy.
