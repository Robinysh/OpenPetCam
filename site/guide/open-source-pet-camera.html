OpenPetCam / Guides

An open-source alternative to Furbo and Petcube

Short answer: OpenPetCam (Open Pet Cam) does the core job of a smart pet camera, live video, alerts and finding your dog when she wanders off, with no subscription and no vendor cloud. In exchange you flash the camera with open firmware and run a small server. If you want treat tossing or a five-minute setup, a commercial camera is the better fit.

What you give up

No treat toss, no app store, and no support line. You need a Thingino-supported pan/tilt camera (the reference is a Cinnado D1), a server with a public IP, and enough comfort with a terminal to run one installer. When something breaks, the fix is a GitHub issue or the built-in agent that drafts a repair, not a phone call.

What you get

Everything stays yours. The camera dials out to your server, so no ports are open at home, and nothing leaves that server. Search is the part commercial cameras rarely do: when the dog is out of view, the camera turns, checks the floor where she usually rests at that hour, and stops on her. Replayed on 56 recorded searches, she was in view at a stop in 48 of them. How the camera learned its room explains why.
